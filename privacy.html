Privacy policy

A plain-English account of what this site does — and does not — collect.

Effective date: November 27, 2026

This page explains what happens when you visit the Just in Case Car Kit site at robieworkshq.github.io/just-in-case-car-kit, which is operated by RobieWorks. Questions: robieworkshq@gmail.com.

A simple, static site

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. There is no account to create, no comment section, and no contact form. We do not run our own analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking scripts on this site, and we do not set first-party cookies.

If you email us

If you write to robieworkshq@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use that only to reply and to handle your request. We do not sell this information.

What our host may collect

Because the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may automatically record ordinary server-log data when you visit, which can include your IP address, browser type, the date and time, and the pages requested. We do not control GitHub’s logging. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Fonts and other third parties on this page

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When those files load, Google may receive your IP address and standard browser information. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

This site may also include a site-verification tag used by Impact so they can confirm we operate the site. That tag does not set a cookie by itself.

Links to other sites

This page links out to other services, including Gumroad (where we sell our own printables), retailer sites such as Walmart, Pinterest, and public information sources such as Ready.gov. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. You can read the policies for Gumroad, Walmart, and Pinterest.

Once shopping links become affiliate links, partners such as Impact and Walmart may set cookies or use similar tools on their own sites to record that you clicked a link and whether a purchase was completed, so a commission can be credited. We do not set those cookies ourselves. You can read the privacy policies of Impact and Walmart for details.

We do not sell your personal information

RobieWorks does not sell the personal information you share with us, including any email you send.

Children

This site offers general information for adults putting together a vehicle kit. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
