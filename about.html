About RobieWorks

A small, independent project for practical family preparedness.

RobieWorks is a small, independent project by Robie, based in Bristol, Tennessee. The aim is practical family preparedness — lists and printables you can actually finish, without a shopping spiral.

Why this car kit exists

The Just in Case Car Kit started as a straightforward question: what should you keep in the vehicle so a breakdown, a winter delay, or a stretch of extreme heat is inconvenient rather than dangerous? The free companion page is a national checklist with starter, lean, and complete builds, plus a clear East Tennessee and mountain winter section, so you can put a kit together on a weekend.

A $7 printable version of the checklist is sold separately on Gumroad, with no shopping links inside, so it can live in the glove box. Prices and store links stay on the free page, where they can be updated.

RobieWorks also makes the Just in Case Family Preparedness Organizer, a home record-keeping printable for contacts, document locations, and the notes a family would need.
